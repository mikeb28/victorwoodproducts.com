Defense Packaging Readiness

Victor Wood Products is entering the federal market from an established commercial manufacturing base of over twenty years.

We build to customer drawings and written specifications as a matter of routine, and we are prepared to fabricate, mark, and document to MIL-STD-2073-1 (military packaging) and MIL-STD-129 (military marking and labeling) requirements, including Levels A, B, and C protection, and to Special Packaging Instructions where cited.

We source ISPM-15 heat-treated lumber under certification and welcome the opportunity to demonstrate compliance through a first article or initial order.
